How it works

What the bot trades, the rules it follows, how it sends you signals, and what to expect.

Market watch

The strategy in one minute

It buys a stock index after a sharp short-term drop, but only while the index is in a long-term uptrend, and sells once it has bounced. It's Larry Connors' 2-day RSI rule, published in 2008, checked once a day on each index's daily chart.

What to trade

Six stock indices, all at once. Your broker may use slightly different names for the same index.

Not currency pairs, gold or oil. The same rules lost money on all seven major currency pairs (−1.1% a year together), barely moved on gold (+0.1% a year) and lost on oil (−0.2% a year). Stock indices rise over time and their sharp dips tend to bounce; currencies don't have that pull. Adding seven more indices (France, Europe 50, Australia, Hong Kong, Spain, Switzerland, Netherlands) also made results worse, because most of them fall together with the ones already traded.

How the bot works

9:05 amTuesday to Saturday: checks yesterday's close and sends your signals. Saturday's message is for Monday's open. 9:35 to 11:05Backup runs in case GitHub delayed a run or prices were late. They stay quiet if the morning message already went out. 8:20 pmEvery day: confirms your replies, reminds you about signals you haven't reported a price for, and updates this site.

Philippine time. GitHub sometimes starts runs 5 to 30 minutes late. If a run fails you get a "⚠ run failed" message, and a morning message that only arrives in the evening starts with a "Late update" warning.

Each morning run

  1. 1
    Wakes up on GitHub
    A free cloud computer runs the bot for a minute or two. Your PC can stay off.
  2. 2
    Reads your Telegram replies
    Prices you filled at, signals you skipped, notes, a new balance.
  3. 3
    Downloads yesterday's daily prices
    Open, high, low and close for the six indices, from Dukascopy's free datafeed (the same data as the backtest).
  4. 4
    Works out the chart indicators
    The 200-day average, the 2-day RSI and the ATR (the average daily range), the same as on a daily MT5 chart.
  5. 5
    Applies the rules and updates the journal
    Yesterday's signals become trades, stop losses hit during the day are recorded, and new buy or sell signals are found.
  6. 6
    Sends your Telegram message
    What to buy or sell with the stop loss and size, what you're holding, or "No action today".
  7. 7
    Saves everything and updates this site
    The journal, trade history and calendar are saved in your GitHub repository.

It doesn't connect to MT5, place trades or watch prices during the day. The only real-time protection is the stop loss you set in MT5, which your broker watches around the clock.

Your routine

BUY message

In the MT5 app, open the index, tap Trade, choose Buy by market, enter the volume and the stop loss from the message. Then reply filled US100 19940 with your price.

SELL message

Close that position in the MT5 app. Reply filled US100 20150 with your price if you like.

Working out lots

Once your broker's contract specs are in the settings, the message gives the exact volume. Until then: lots = size per point ÷ the index's contract size (in MT5: hold the symbol → Specification), rounded down.

Warnings

Borderline means a signal only just met a rule, and close call means it only just missed one. The bot's prices can differ slightly from your broker's, so check your MT5 daily chart for those.

Entering an hour or two after the message is fine: on 2026's trades it cost about 0.05% per trade on average. Skipping signals costs far more.

Tested on July 2014 to October 2026 with realistic costs and 0.5% risk per trade. Treat these numbers as a guide, not a promise:

  • Results depend on the market: about +2% a year in 2014-2020 and about +7% a year in 2021-2026.
  • Some years are flat or slightly down. The worst full year was about −5%.
  • Simply holding an S&P 500 index fund made more (+11.5% a year), with much bigger drops (−33%).
  • Your broker's real spreads and fees decide a lot, because the average trade is only about +0.5%.
